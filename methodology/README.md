# Search, screening and post-window tracking / 检索、筛选与窗口后追踪

## English

**Screening counts.** `prisma_counts.csv` holds the stage counts and the
exclusion reasons behind the PRISMA flow diagram: 252 records after database
search, 233 after deduplication, 172 after title and abstract screening, and 121
after full-text assessment. The figure itself is drawn with the manuscript and
is not duplicated in this repository.

**Reconstructed queries.** The per-database queries shown in Appendix B of the
paper are reproducible reconstructions from the keyword families and each
platform's query syntax. The original search logs are not archived, and the
per-database hit counts do not correspond directly to the 121 included papers.

**Post-window tracking.** The search window closes in May 2026.
`2026-preprint-tracking.md` lists the preprints that appeared afterwards
(MileStone, AutoPass, DualScale) and states how each is treated in the survey.

## 中文

**筛选计数。** `prisma_counts.csv` 保存 PRISMA 流程图背后的阶段计数与排除理由：
数据库检索后 252 条、去重后 233 条、标题摘要筛选后 172 条、全文资格审查后 121 条。
图形本身随论文正文绘制，不在本仓库重复存放。

**检索式。** 论文附录 B 的逐库检索式为按关键词族与各平台检索语法重建的复核版本；
原始检索日志未存档，逐库命中数不直接对应最终纳入的 121 篇。

**窗口后追踪。** 检索窗口截至 2026 年 5 月。`2026-preprint-tracking.md` 列出此后
出现的预印本（MileStone、AutoPass、DualScale）及各自的处理方式。
